Three generations of precision work.

What started as a small tool-and-die shop is now a trusted subcontractor to automotive and industrial OEMs across Belgium and neighbouring markets.

Norvantec Precision was founded in 1979 by Albertine Bourdette, who opened a small tool-and-die workshop in the Charleroi region to serve the regional automotive-parts boom of the era. What began as subcontract machining for a handful of local clients has grown into a 32-person operation supplying structural and precision components to Tier-2 automotive suppliers and industrial-equipment OEMs.

The company remains family-owned. Today's leadership includes the founder's grandson and great-granddaughter, continuing a hands-on approach to quality that's carried the business through four decades of industry change.

We're currently investing in our next chapter: a modernised ERP rollout across production and quality systems, and pursuit of AS9100 certification to open the door to aerospace subcontracting work.
